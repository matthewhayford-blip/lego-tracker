Why retirement matters

LEGO produces a set for a few years, then stops. Once retail stock clears, the only supply is whatever people already own. Sealed sets that were easy to buy at {{ market.symbol }}50 become hard to find at any price. Knowing the date is the difference between paying RRP and paying a premium.

Every set here has a page showing its retirement date, current {{ market.adjective }} prices where we have them, and a model of what holding one sealed is worth, with the assumptions written down so you can disagree with them.
